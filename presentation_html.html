Titanic Facts

The Ship

The Legacy

Sank on April 15, 1912 (113 years ago).

Over 1500 of the approx. 2200 people on board perished.

Led to several international maritime safety regulations.

03

Methodology: Preprocessing

Data handling choices were justified by Exploratory Data Analysis:

  • Skewness: Right-skewed Fare normalized via log1p transformation.
  • Missingness: Median imputation (Numeric) and "Missing" labels (Categorical).
  • Consistency: Fixed Seed 42 applied to all randomized operations for strict reproducibility.
04

Model 1: k-Nearest Neighbors

k-NN achieved high predictive performance through scaling and PCA-based dimensionality reduction.

Performance Metrics:

07

Discussion: Generalization Gap

Why Strategy B (Engineering) underperformed:

  • Discretization Loss: Binning Age/Fare discarded detail trees use natively.
  • Redundancy: New interaction terms duplicated existing augmented signals.
  • Instability: Manual features overfitted to partition noise in cross-validation.
